# Code of Conduct

We want participation in REHEARSE to be safe, respectful and useful.

Be considerate, disagree with ideas rather than people, protect confidential event information, and make room for contributors with different levels of technical and event-production experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private material are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and communication that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
